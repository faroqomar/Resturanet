## Design Context

### Users

A single restaurant owner working on one Mac who needs to record invoices and payments quickly and understand the financial position without complex accounting terminology.

### Brand personality

Calm, clear, and dependable. The numbers should give the owner a sense of control rather than confusion.

### Aesthetic direction

A light, practical bilingual interface. Arabic uses RTL and English uses LTR. Warm sand backgrounds, dark ink, olive for healthy states, brick for losses, and amber for warnings. Large readable type, comfortable spacing, and strong contrast.

### Design principles

- Important numbers should be readable at a glance.
- Daily entry is faster than advanced browsing.
- A warning explains both the problem and the next action.
- No state depends on color alone.
- Advanced details appear only when needed.
- User-entered names and notes are never translated or rewritten.
